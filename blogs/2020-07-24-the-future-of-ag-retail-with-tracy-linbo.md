The ag industry and ag retail has seen an accelerated change in the last 5 years and more specifically, in the last 5 months. The retail landscape is changing, but what will that continue to look like? During this episode, Anna Cardoze chats with Tracy Linbo, industry veteran and EVP Chief Commercial Officer of Growers Edge on the future of ag retail and how digital will continue to play a role in the space.
